Last Call RP

Gang Bible

Rules are subject to change at any time

All citizens are expected to follow server rules and gang rules apply to city recognized gangs.

Gang Registration:

  • Must have a minimum of 5 active members.
  • Network with gangs in the city for info and get your name out there in a positive way.        
  • Have an active presence in the city.
  • Submit a gang application in the city discord.
  • Wait for staff to contact you after the application is either denied or accepted.
  • Do not DM staff for an update.
  • You may only have one gang character.

Once Accepted:

  • Staff will contact you in discord to start the process.
  • Your gang will have an in city meet up with the in city gang team presence in an RP fashion.
  • You will be given specific instructions.
  • Gangs will have a 7 day trial period where your organization’s activity will be highly monitored before you become in-city recognized by the gang system.

Officially recognized gangs:

  • You will follow in city steps of gang creation.
  • Gangs will be required to be part of the official Last Call RP gang discord.
  • All members on the roster must be in this discord.
  • Members must be in city a minimum of seven (7) days before joining the gang discord or the gangs roster.
  • Gangs will be required to keep an accurate and up to date roster. Failing to do so could result in a gang strike.


Denied Gangs:

  • Denial can be the result of many things. Please ensure you followed the application instructions. Gang staff will give you a reason behind the denial. If denied a gang may reapply in 5 days. Be sure to contribute to the city’s growing RP and have an active presence.


Groups will continue to be monitored in their early stages. Gang staff expects meaningful RP and relationships built. Any pattern of gun play or continued poor RP will be addressed by the team and disbandment is at the discretion of Gang Team.

Gang Activity:

  • Leader(s) are expected to be active.
  • A minimum of 5 active members are required to hold official status.
  • If membership falls below the 5, the org has 7 days to bring the numbers back to 5 or could result in disbandment. Please reach out to the Gang Team to discuss if this issue arises either in a ticket or designated section of gang discord.
  • Gang activity will be monitored. If gangs become inactive they will be given a warning about lack of activity to improve this. If inactivity continues disbandment will be at the discretion of The Gang Team.
  • Max roster is 15 members. Hang arounds are allowed but if not on the official roster cannot be involved in gang beef or official war.
  • Gang Characters must be in the city for a week before joining a gang.
  • Gang Cool down. If a gang member leaves or gets kicked you will have to wait 1 week before joining a new gang.

Penalties:

  • Leaders are responsible for the conduct of their gang members. A striking system for gangs is in place.
  • The first gang strike will be a warning that will allow the Gangs to make adjustments.
  • Second strike will result in gang privileges being temporarily revoked.
  • Third strike will result in disbandment.
  • Gang Team reserves the right to bypass the strike system if they have found the organization to be unfit for the community.

  • Groups if disbanded(unless permanently removed from community) may reapply after 60 days with no members having any severe infractions in the city.
  • Gang strikes have the ability to be removed after 60 days following no repeat infractions or additional strikes issued.

Gang Colors:

  • Gangs may not “beef” over generic colors or standard items in the clothing store.
  • Custom clothing specific to gangs will only be worn by that group. Groups cannot impersonate other groups.
  • Gang colors must be worn during Gang Team approved wars.
  • Gangs are allowed to “blackout” or remove colors while doing jobs, selling drugs or RP specific reasons to be unrecognizable.
  • If someone is found to be wearing your custom items RP it out. If it continues, reach out to staff.

Gang Cars:

  • Gang cars belong to the gang. If a member is to leave a gang the car must be returned to the gang. All assets donated to LCRP are property of LCRP and if a gang disbands the server has the right to add the cars to the civilian dealerships.

Turf Regulations:

  • Gangs can claim up to 3 turfs without in game penalties.
  • Taking of turf should not involve “offline selling” or deliberately to avoid conflict.
  • Taking turf should have meaningful RP interactions and build up. Taking turf “just cause” without valid scenes will be addressed by gang staff and result in the return of the turf to the original owner.
  • There is no tagging of privately owned businesses without valid RP or posting of “scenes.”

Gang Discord Conduct:

  • Be respectful to others as well as staff.
  • Use of slurs or hateful speech will result in disbandment and removal.
  • Rosters are to be kept up to date.
  • Members can be placed on a discord timeout if conduct is unbecoming but doesn’t warrant a ban.
  • No discord “trash talking” keep everything OOC and remember what happens IC stays IC.

Gang War:

  • Gang wars must be approved by gang staff which will involve a meeting between gangs to set rules of war.
  • If a side requests “peace” it must be accepted.
  • A gang that was at war is unable to beef or antagonize the other group into another beef for a minimum of 14 days.  
  • Colors must be worn at all times during a declared war. Any member found to be out of colors to avoid war the org will be subject to gang discipline.
  • Ambushes must allow some level of RP interaction before immediate violence.

Gang Scene Limits:

  • Gang beef cannot exceed 5v5 with one lookout not involved in any shooting.
  • Selling of drugs on a single turf should not exceed 5 people.
  • Gangs can sell on multiple turfs at one time but never exceeding 5 people.
  • Scene limits that are server specific should be followed and reference those in the LCRP City Rules.
  • There should be no selling on gang turfs during an active shootout between gangs.

Gang Beefs:

  • There is to be no nonstop retaliation cycles or repeated attacks without a minimum of 30 minute cooldown.
  • Proper initiation before all shooting begins. Poor initiation will be subject to gang discipline.
  • Gangs should fear police presence and act accordingly.
  • Green zones are to be respected. They should also not be used as a “hiding” zone.  
  • Extended gang beef exceeding 7 days could be subject to elevation to war terms. Approval by Gang Staff required. RP should have an end goal and not needless shootouts.

Alliances:

  • Alliances should develop through natural RP.
  • Betrayals, coups, and internal conflict are encouraged through proper RP.
  • Avoid OOC influence on gang politics.

Respect Roleplay First:

  • Quality RP always comes before winning.
  • Do not break character during active scenes.
  • Do not trash talk over downed bodies.
  • Avoid “win mentality” behavior.
  • Create meaningful stories, rivalries, alliances and consequences.

Member Conduct:

  • Gang members represent their organization at all times.
  • Excessive toxicity, harassment, racism, or OOC drama will not be tolerated.
  • Members must follow all server rules in addition to gang rules.
  • LQRP will be subject to gang discipline.

Kidnapping & Hostages

  • Hostage RP must be realistic and meaningful.
  • Do not repeatedly kidnap the same players to farm RP or money.
  • Hostages must be given the opportunities to respond to roleplay.

Fear RP:

  • Gang members must value their lives.
  • Running into impossible odds without concern is unrealistic.
  • Players should react appropriately to threats, injuries and consequences.
  • RP true to your organization classification. I.e street gangs would be more violent where mafias would be more methodical. Regardless, everything should have valid RP.

Criminal Activity:

  • Gang crimes should create RP for everyone involved. Not every scene should be straight to chase. Scenes should have valid interactions with officers.
  • Constant robbery grinding without valid interactions is not allowed.
  • Vehicle ramming solely to disable opponents' cars without RP is not allowed.
  • Pursuit baiting is not allowed.

OOC Conduct:

  • Do not use discord, streams or outside information for gang advantages.
  • Metagaming or stream sniping are bannable offenses.
  • No “waking up” the crew via discord for things happening in the city to increase numbers. All scenes should be fair based on activity and not just “fly-ins.”

Leadership Expectations:

  • Gang leaders are responsible for the member's behavior.
  • Leaders should encourage story-driven RP and community involvement.
  • Failure to control toxic behavior may result in gang strikes or removal.

Punishments:

  • Violations may result in:
  • Warnings
  • Gang strikes
  • Loss of official gang status
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent bans

Gang Tickets:

  • Any rule breaks should be brought to the attention of Gang Team by opening a ticket in the discord. Please provide any bodycam or evidence to support your ticket.
  • Staff is to be respected in tickets.

Final Rule:

  • If your actions ruin RP for others, staff may intervene even if the situation is not specifically listed above. The goal is to create immersive, enjoyable and long-lasting stories for the entire community. This is a roleplay server above all. This is not your personal gang server or GTA online. All interactions should be meaningful and not focused on gunplay over roleplay.

Rules are subject to change at any time. Please familiarize yourself often for any changes.
